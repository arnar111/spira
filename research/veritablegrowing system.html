1. Introduction & System Overview

The Véritable SMART (and its Bluetooth-enabled sibling, the Véritable CONNECT) is a French-designed, compact hydroponic indoor garden manufactured by Veritable-Gardens SAS in Saint-Priest, France. Unlike traditional pot-and-soil growing, the Véritable system uses a proprietary Lingot pod system — pre-seeded, nutrient-embedded biodegradable cartridges that eliminate the guesswork of germination, planting depth, and initial fertilization. The system is designed for countertop use in kitchens, living rooms, or offices, and operates fully autonomously for weeks at a time with minimal user intervention.

The core value proposition of the Véritable SMART is set-and-forget simplicity combined with year-round production. The built-in LED grow light runs on a timer, the water reservoir feeds plants via capillary wicks, and the Lingots contain everything a plant needs for its first 3–6 months of life. For users in climates like Iceland — where Spíra is primarily targeted — the Véritable SMART offers a compelling complement to seasonal outdoor growing: fresh herbs, leafy greens, baby vegetables, and even compact fruiting plants like cherry tomatoes or strawberries can be harvested throughout the dark winter months when outdoor gardening is impossible.

This report covers every aspect of owning and operating a Véritable SMART system, from unboxing to daily maintenance, with particular attention to the data points needed to integrate it as a first-class growing environment within the Spíra app. Whether a Spíra user is selecting “Indoor Pot,” “Outdoor Pot,” “Shower,” “Growth Tent,” or now “Véritable SMART” as their setup type, the app needs precise parameters to deliver relevant AI advice, timely notifications, and accurate care schedules.

2. The Lingot System: How It Works

The Lingot is the heart of the Véritable growing system. It is a rectangular, biodegradable pod approximately 5 × 15 cm in size, containing a carefully engineered growing medium, pre-sown seeds, and a time-released nutrient charge. Understanding the Lingot's composition and lifecycle is essential for any grower — and for building accurate care logic into the Spíra app.

2.1 Lingot Composition & Nutrient Delivery

Each Lingot consists of three functional layers. The outer shell is made from biodegradable plant-based materials that hold the pod's shape while allowing root penetration. The growing medium inside is a blend of peat, coconut fiber, and perlite — a soilless mix that provides excellent aeration, moisture retention, and root anchorage. The nutrient core contains a controlled-release organic fertilizer charge (typically 4–5 grams) that gradually breaks down over the Lingot's lifetime, supplying nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, and micronutrients to the plant.

The Lingot is placed into a grow basket that sits partially submerged in the water tank. Two capillary wicks extend from the bottom of the Lingot into the water, drawing moisture upward through passive capillary action. This eliminates the need for pumps, electricity for irrigation, or manual watering schedules — the plant takes exactly what it needs. The wicks are made of woven textile fibers and typically last 6–12 months before requiring replacement. When the tank runs low, a float-based visual indicator drops, alerting the user to refill.

Key Lingot Facts for Spíra Integration
  • Dimensions: ~5 cm × 15 cm × 4 cm (fits standard Véritable grow baskets)
  • Weight: ~80–100 grams dry
  • Nutrient charge: 4–5 g organic controlled-release fertilizer
  • NPK analysis: approximately 4.3–2.1–6.6 (varies by variety)
  • pH range: 5.5–6.5 (slightly acidic, optimal for nutrient uptake)
  • Lifetime: 3–6 months depending on plant variety
  • Seed count: multiple seeds per Lingot (thinned to 1–3 plants after germination)
  • Compostable: Lingot shell and spent medium are biodegradable

3. Water Management

Water management is the single most important recurring task for a Véritable SMART owner. While the system is designed to be low-maintenance, neglecting the water tank is the most common cause of poor plant performance. This section provides precise guidance on how often to refill, how much water to add, and how to maintain water quality.

3.1 Tank Capacity & Refill Schedule

The Véritable SMART features a 2-liter water reservoir (the Exky SMART has a 1-liter tank). Under normal growing conditions with 4 Lingots installed, the tank will need refilling approximately every 7 to 14 days. The exact interval depends on several factors: plant size and transpiration rate, ambient temperature and humidity, the number of Lingots installed, and the plant varieties being grown. Large, fruiting plants like cherry tomatoes or bell peppers consume significantly more water than compact herbs like thyme or chives.

The system includes a visual water level indicator — a small float that drops as the water level decreases. When the float is at its lowest position, the tank is nearly empty and should be refilled immediately. For users who want more precise monitoring, the Véritable CONNECT (Bluetooth-enabled) sends a push notification to the mobile app when the water level drops below a threshold. However, even CONNECT users should visually verify the indicator periodically, as the app notification relies on the same float sensor.

Critical Water Management Rules
  • Never let the tank run completely dry — even for a few hours. Dry wicks lose capillary action and may not re-saturate properly, stressing or killing plants.
  • Use room-temperature water (18–22°C). Cold water shocks roots; hot water damages them.
  • Fill to just below the max line — overfilling can submerge the grow baskets and cause root rot.
  • Tap water is acceptable in most regions. If your tap water is very hard or heavily chlorinated, let it sit uncovered for 24 hours before use, or use filtered water.

3.2 Water Quality & Wick Maintenance

Over time, mineral deposits from tap water and organic matter from the Lingots can accumulate in the water tank and on the wicks. This buildup can reduce capillary efficiency and create an environment for algae or bacterial growth. A simple monthly cleaning routine prevents these issues.

To clean the tank, remove all Lingots and set them aside (roots can tolerate brief air exposure). Empty the tank completely and rinse it with warm water. For stubborn deposits, a dilute solution of white vinegar (1 part vinegar to 4 parts water) can be used — rinse thoroughly afterward. The wicks should be inspected monthly; if they appear clogged, discolored, or slimy, they should be replaced. Replacement wicks are available from Véritable retailers. Never use soap or detergent on any part of the system, as residue can harm plants.

Algae growth in the tank is a common issue, especially if the garden is placed in a bright location where light penetrates the water reservoir. Algae are generally harmless to plants but can clog wicks and create unpleasant odors. The best prevention is to keep the tank opaque (the Véritable SMART tank is dark-colored for this reason) and to change the water completely every 2–3 weeks rather than just topping it up.

4. Nutrition & Fertilization

One of the most common questions from new Véritable owners is: “Do I need to add fertilizer, or does the Lingot have everything?” The answer depends on the plant variety, the age of the Lingot, and the grower's goals. This section explains when and how to supplement the Lingot's built-in nutrients.

4.1 Proprietary Lingot vs. DIY Nutrients

The Lingot's embedded nutrient charge is designed to support the plant for its entire commercial lifespan — typically 3 to 6 months. For herbs and leafy greens, this built-in nutrition is usually sufficient. However, fruiting plants (cherry tomatoes, peppers, strawberries, eggplant) are heavy feeders and will benefit from supplemental fertilization once the initial Lingot nutrients begin to deplete, usually around 8–10 weeks after planting.

Signs that a plant needs additional nutrition include: yellowing lower leaves (nitrogen deficiency), poor flowering or fruit set (phosphorus/potassium deficiency), stunted growth despite adequate light and water, and pale or discolored foliage. If these symptoms appear and the Lingot is more than 2 months old, supplemental feeding is warranted.

For growers who want to reuse Lingot baskets with their own seeds and growing medium (a common practice after the initial Lingot is spent), a complete hydroponic nutrient solution becomes essential. The Lingot basket provides the physical structure, but all nutrition must come from the water. In this scenario, a two-part or three-part hydroponic fertilizer designed for recirculating systems is recommended.

5. LED Lighting & AdaptLight Technology

The Véritable SMART uses a proprietary AdaptLight LED system that is arguably the most sophisticated lighting technology in the consumer indoor gardening market. Understanding how it works — and how it adapts to plant growth stages — is essential for maximizing yields and for modeling light parameters in the Spíra app.

5.1 Light Spectrum & Photoperiod by Growth Stage

The AdaptLight system consists of an array of OSRAM LEDs optimized for horticultural applications. The LEDs emit light in two primary wavelength bands: blue light (450–495 nm) and red light (620–750 nm), with some far-red extension. Blue light drives vegetative growth, leaf development, and compact plant structure. Red light stimulates flowering, fruiting, and stem elongation. The ratio between these two spectra shifts automatically as the plant grows, a feature Véritable calls Adapt'Light.

On the Véritable CONNECT model, this spectrum adaptation is controlled via the app. The user selects a growth stage mode (Germination, Growth, or Flowering), and the LED controller adjusts the red:blue ratio and photoperiod accordingly. On the Véritable SMART (non-Bluetooth), the light runs a fixed default program that provides a balanced spectrum suitable for most plants — approximately 16 hours on, 8 hours off, with a moderate red:blue ratio.

5.2 Growth Stage Modes (APP & CONNECT)

The Véritable CONNECT app offers three preset lighting modes that simplify the spectrum management for users who do not want to manually adjust red:blue ratios. These modes map roughly to the growth stages described above and are selected when setting up a new Lingot in the app.

Germination Mode is optimized for the first 1–2 weeks after planting a Lingot. It provides high blue light to encourage compact, sturdy seedlings and prevent leggy growth. The photoperiod is 16 hours. Growth Mode is the default for leafy greens and herbs throughout their lifecycle. It provides a balanced spectrum with slightly more red than blue, promoting lush foliage. The photoperiod remains at 16 hours. Flowering Mode is designed for fruiting plants (tomatoes, peppers, strawberries) once they reach maturity. It shifts the spectrum heavily toward red and reduces the photoperiod to 12 hours, mimicking the shorter days of late summer that trigger reproductive development in many plants.

The Véritable SMART (without Bluetooth) does not offer app-controlled mode switching. Instead, it runs a fixed program that provides a compromise spectrum suitable for a mix of herbs and leafy greens. Users growing fruiting plants on the SMART model may achieve better results by manually timing an external timer to reduce light hours to 12 once plants reach flowering size, or by upgrading to the CONNECT model for full spectrum control.

6. The Véritable Connect App

The Véritable CONNECT model adds Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) connectivity and a companion mobile app that transforms the indoor garden from a passive appliance into an interactive growing companion. While the Véritable SMART lacks Bluetooth, understanding the app's functionality is valuable for Spíra integration — many of the same features (plant tracking, care reminders, harvest logging) can and should be replicated within Spíra for SMART users.

6.1 App Features & Functionality

The Véritable CONNECT app (available for iOS and Android) provides a structured interface for managing the garden. Upon first launch, the app guides the user through a setup wizard: selecting the garden model, naming it, and choosing growth stage modes for each Lingot slot. The home screen displays a visual representation of the garden with status indicators for water level, light mode, and plant age.

The core features include: Water level monitoring — a real-time reading from the tank's float sensor, with push notifications when levels drop; Light mode control — switching between Germination, Growth, and Flowering modes for each slot independently; Plant tracking — logging planting dates, variety names, and growth stage transitions; Harvest logging — recording harvest quantities and dates (though the app's harvest tracking is basic compared to Spíra's journal system); and Tips & guidance — contextual care advice based on the selected plant variety and growth stage.

User reviews of the app are mixed. The app has a 3.4-star rating on the App Store and a similar rating on Google Play. Common complaints include Bluetooth pairing difficulties (especially on Android), occasional crashes, and a desire for more detailed plant care information. Strengths include the intuitive garden visualization and the convenience of remote light mode switching. For Spíra, these pain points represent an opportunity: by offering robust, offline-first tracking with AI-powered advice (via Rós), Spíra can provide a superior experience even for CONNECT owners who may prefer Spíra's journal and companion features over Véritable's native app.

6.2 Bluetooth Connectivity & Setup

Pairing the Véritable CONNECT with the app requires a one-time setup process. The garden must be powered on, Bluetooth enabled on the phone, and the app granted location permissions (BLE scanning requires location access on both iOS and Android). The app scans for nearby Véritable devices and displays them in a list. Once selected, the pairing is automatic and persists across app launches.

Connection range is typical for BLE: approximately 10 meters line-of-sight, less through walls. The app does not require an internet connection for basic functions (light control, water monitoring), but some features (plant variety database updates, cloud backup) require connectivity. The Véritable SMART model lacks Bluetooth entirely, so none of these app features are available. However, the SMART's physical button on the lamp arm allows manual toggling of the light on/off, and the light runs its internal timer autonomously once set.

Common Problems & Solutions
  • Leggy seedlings: Insufficient light. Ensure LED is on for 16 hours daily. Move garden away from competing natural light sources that may confuse the plant's photoperiod.
  • Yellow lower leaves: Nitrogen deficiency. If Lingot is older than 8 weeks, begin supplemental feeding with a nitrogen-rich fertilizer (e.g., Bio-Grow or FloraGro).
  • No flowers on fruiting plants: Light spectrum may be too blue, or photoperiod too long. Switch to Flowering mode (CONNECT) or reduce light to 12 hours manually (SMART).
  • Algae in tank: Light penetrating water. Ensure tank is not overfilled; clean tank weekly; keep garden away from strong ambient light.
  • Wicks not drawing water: Wicks may be air-locked or clogged. Remove and re-soak wicks in water for 30 minutes. Replace if problem persists.
  • Mold on Lingot surface: Too humid, poor air circulation. Reduce ambient humidity if possible; ensure room has some airflow (gentle fan).

11. Key Takeaways

The Véritable SMART is a well-engineered, low-maintenance hydroponic system that fills a distinct niche in the indoor growing ecosystem. Unlike DIY hydroponics, it requires no assembly, no plumbing, and no deep technical knowledge. Unlike soil-based growing, it eliminates the mess, pests, and watering guesswork. For Spíra users in Iceland and similar climates, it represents an ideal winter companion to seasonal outdoor growing — providing fresh herbs, greens, and even small fruits during the months when outdoor gardening is impossible.

The key operational principles are simple: keep the tank filled, let the light run, and harvest regularly. The Lingot system handles germination, initial nutrition, and planting depth automatically. Fruiting plants require supplemental nutrients after approximately 8 weeks, and the CONNECT model's app-controlled spectrum tuning offers a meaningful yield advantage for tomatoes, peppers, and strawberries. Maintenance is minimal — a tank clean every 2–3 weeks, wick inspection monthly, and Lingot replacement every 3–6 months.

For Spíra integration, the critical data model elements are: (1) a veritable_smart setup type with tank capacity, LED specs, and passive-wick watering method; (2) a notification engine tuned to hydroponic rhythms (water checks every 3 days, tank refills every 7–14 days, cleaning every 14 days); (3) Lingot crop presets with accurate sprouting, harvest, and lifespan timelines for the ~30 available varieties; and (4) AI prompting context that distinguishes Véritable care (passive hydroponics, built-in nutrients, capillary watering) from soil-based setups. With this data in place, Spíra can deliver precise, relevant guidance to Véritable growers — whether they are nurturing their first basil Lingot or managing a full 4-slot garden of cherry tomatoes and strawberries through an Icelandic winter.
